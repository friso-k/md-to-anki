What are the nine stages of shamatha practice?

---

The nine stages describe progressively stabilizing attention:

1. Placement of the mind: deliberately placing attention on the object.
2. Continuous placement: staying with the object for brief stretches.
3. Repeated placement: noticing distraction and returning again and again.
4. Close placement: attention stays mostly on the object, with fewer gaps.
5. Taming: overcoming coarse dullness and agitation.
6. Pacifying: calming subtler restlessness and resistance.
7. Thoroughly pacifying: resolving subtle faults before they grow.
8. One-pointedness: attention remains steady with little effort.
9. Balanced placement: stable, clear attention rests naturally and effortlessly.
