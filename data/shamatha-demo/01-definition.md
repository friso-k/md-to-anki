# What does shamatha mean?

---

## Meaning

Shamatha means **calm abiding**.

## Purpose

It is a meditation practice for developing attention that is:

* Stable
* Clear
* Relaxed
* Less easily distracted

## How is shamatha practised?

### Choose an object

Rest your attention on a meditation object, such as:

* The breath at the nose
* The movement of the abdomen
* A sound
* A visual object

### Notice distraction

When your attention moves toward a thought, feeling, sound, or sensation, notice that you became distracted.

### Return gently

Let go of the distraction and calmly return your attention to the meditation object.

## What is the goal?

The goal is not to eliminate every thought. The goal is to notice distraction sooner and return without frustration.

With practice, attention becomes increasingly steady, clear, and effortless.
